Featured favourites

Most popular air-fryer dishes

Classic

Crispy Chicken Nuggets

  • Chicken breast or nuggets
  • Breadcrumbs or panko
  • Salt, pepper, paprika
Method

Coat lightly, spray with oil, and air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, turning once.

Crowd pleaser

Golden Chips

  • Potatoes cut into chips
  • Olive oil or spray oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Method

Soak briefly, dry well, toss in oil, and cook at 200°C for 15–20 minutes, shaking halfway.

Quick bite

Air-Fried Chicken Wings

  • Chicken wings
  • Garlic powder
  • Hot sauce or BBQ glaze
Method

Season well, air fry at 190°C for 18–22 minutes, then finish with your sauce.

Healthy option

Air-Fried Salmon Fillets

  • Salmon fillets
  • Lemon juice
  • Herbs and garlic
Method

Brush with oil and seasoning, cook at 180°C for 10–12 minutes until flaky and golden.

Cheesy

Air-Fried Cheese Toasties

  • Bread
  • Cheddar or mozzarella
  • Butter or garlic spread
Method

Build the sandwich and air fry at 180°C for 6–8 minutes until crisp and melty.

Special

Air-Fried Trangoli Pasta

  • Cooked pasta shapes
  • Tomato sauce or pesto
  • Cheese and herbs
Method

Mix pasta with sauce and cheese, place in a shallow tray, and air fry at 180°C until hot and crisp at the edges.

Air-fryer basics

Little tricks for perfect results

1. Don’t overcrowd

Leave space around each piece so the hot air can circulate and crisp everything evenly.

2. Spray lightly

A quick spray of oil helps with colour and crunch without making food greasy.

3. Shake or turn

Flip chips, nuggets, and wings halfway through for a more even golden finish.

4. Preheat when needed

For extra crispness, preheat the air fryer for a few minutes before cooking.
